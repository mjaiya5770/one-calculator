Lawn Care Cost Estimator

Lawn care is sold per visit but felt per season. This estimator prices both views: the per-visit mowing rate for your lawn size, and the full season with fertilisation and leaf cleanups itemised — the annual number nobody quotes you.

Lawn size is the grass, not the lot — subtract the house, driveway and beds. An 8,000 sq ft lawn is a typical suburban quarter-acre lot's actual turf. Weekly mowing in season is 4–5 visits a month.

How it works

Per-visit mowing follows size tiers: $50 up to 5,000 sq ft, $65 to 10,000, $85 to a half acre, then about +$4 per additional 1,000 sq ft. The season is visits × months. Fertilisation applications price at 1.4× a mowing visit each; a leaf cleanup at 3× a visit (it is a crew-hour job, not a mow). The monthly average spreads the season across its months — the honest way to compare a service contract against DIY weekends.

Estimates only, typical US ranges for standard flat lawns. Slopes, heavy edging, gated access, and overgrowth surcharges move real quotes. Aeration, overseeding and irrigation are separate services — the Grass Seed Estimator prices the seed for a DIY overseed.

Method & worked example

Method: Mowing per visit by lawn tier: $50 to 5,000 sq ft, $65 to 10,000, $85 to a half acre, then +$4 per extra 1,000 sq ft. Season = per-visit × visits/mo × months; fertilisation = applications × 1.4 × per-visit; leaf cleanup = cleanups × 3 × per-visit.

Worked example: An 8,000 sq ft lawn: $65/visit; 32 visits + 4 fertilisations + 1 leaf cleanup = $2,639 for the season, about $330/month across 8 months.

When to use this

  • You are hiring mowing service and want the per-visit fair rate by size.
  • You want the whole season's cost before signing anything.
  • You are comparing DIY equipment against the service's annual number.

How to use it, step by step

  1. Enter the lawn's actual turf area, not the lot size.
  2. Set visits per month and season length for your climate.
  3. Add fertilisation applications and leaf cleanups if you buy them.
  4. Read the season total and the monthly average together.

Another worked example

A 4,000 sq ft lawn, mowing only, 4 visits a month over a 7-month season: $50 per visit × 28 visits = $1,400 for the season, exactly $200 a month across it. Small lawns are priced by the truck roll, which is why the per-visit rate only falls to $50 and no lower.

Frequently asked questions

How much does lawn care cost for an 8,000 sq ft lawn?

Mowing is $65 per visit; a full 8-month season at 4 visits a month with 4 fertilisation applications and 1 leaf cleanup totals $2,639 — about $330 a month across the season. Mowing alone is $2,080 of it.

How much for a small 4,000 sq ft lawn?

$50 per visit; mowing-only over a 7-month season is $1,400, about $200 a month. Small lawns hit the service minimum fast — the truck roll dominates the price, which is why neighbours on the same route pay less.

Is a season contract or per-visit better?

Contracts usually price the same visits with the admin smoothed — the savings lever is not the contract form, it is the visit count. Dropping to biweekly in slow-growth months (with the lawn's agreement) cuts the mowing line by a quarter or more.

What does fertilisation add?

At 1.4× a mow per application, four applications on the default lawn add $364 a season. It is the highest-margin line in lawn care and the easiest to DIY — a spreader and the Grass Seed Estimator's lawn math cover most of it.

Mowing service or buy a mower?

A $65 visit × 32 visits is $2,080 a year against a good mower at $400–$600 plus your Saturday hours. The service wins on time and stripes; DIY wins on cost after year one — if the Saturdays were actually free.

🌱 Overseeding that lawn this fall?

The Grass Seed Estimator counts pounds and bags by grass type — new lawn or overseed rates — so the DIY half of lawn care is priced as precisely as the service half.

What does aeration and overseeding cost?

Aeration typically runs 1.5–2× a mowing visit; overseeding adds seed cost by the Grass Seed Estimator's math plus labour. Fall is the season for both in cool-season lawns.

Is it cheaper to pay per visit or per season?

Season contracts usually equal the same visits summed, with billing smoothed. The genuine savings lever is visit count in slow-growth months and route density — neighbours hiring the same crew pay less per stop.
