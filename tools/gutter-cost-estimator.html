Gutter Cost Estimator

Gutter quotes hide their anatomy: the run, the downspouts, and the guards are three different prices wearing one coat. This estimator separates them — per-foot by material, downspouts each, guards as the optional line they are.

Measure along the eaves, not the house footprint — gable ends have no gutters. The default 148 ft is a typical 1-story home's two eaves plus returns. One downspout per roughly 35–40 ft of run is the drainage rule of thumb.

How it works

Installed per-foot bands for the run: vinyl $6–$10, aluminium $9–$14, steel $11–$17, copper $28–$42. Downspouts price per each at $120–$200 installed (they are elbows, straps and labour, not just tube). Guards add $6–$10 per foot for quality mesh/hood systems — the foam inserts at $2/ft are a different, shorter-lived product. On the default home the run is $1,332–$2,072 of the $1,812–$2,872 total; downspouts are the second line nobody budgets.

Estimates only, typical US ranges for 5–6 in K-style gutters on a reachable eave. Two-story access, fascia repair, and underground drain tie-ins price separately. Copper is a 50-year architectural choice priced like one.

Method & worked example

Method: Total = run (lineal ft × material band: vinyl $6–10, aluminium $9–14, steel $11–17, copper $28–42) + downspouts at $120–200 each + optional guards at $6–10 per lineal ft.

Worked example: 148 ft of aluminium with 4 downspouts and no guards: $1,332–$2,072 run + $480–$800 downspouts = $1,812–$2,872 installed.

When to use this

  • You are replacing gutters and a quote bundles guards invisibly.
  • You are adding gutters to a house that never had them.
  • Downspout count is in dispute — you want the pricing anatomy.

How to use it, step by step

  1. Measure the eave runs in lineal feet (gables have no gutters).
  2. Pick the material.
  3. Count downspouts: one per 35–40 ft of run.
  4. Toggle guards and watch their share of the total — decide with open eyes.

Another worked example

120 ft of aluminium with 3 downspouts and guards fitted: run $1,080–$1,680; downspouts $360–$600; guards $720–$1,200; total $2,160–$3,480, mid-band $2,820. The guards are roughly a third of the job — under open sky they are optional, under oaks they are the point.

Frequently asked questions

How much do gutters cost for a typical house?

For 148 ft of seamless aluminium with 4 downspouts: $1,812–$2,872 installed, mid-band about $2,342. Add guards and the same job runs about $2,700–$4,350 — guards are a third of a full quote, which is why they should always be their own line.

What do 120 ft of gutters with guards cost?

Aluminium with 3 downspouts and guards: $2,160–$3,480. The guards alone are $720–$1,200 of it — worth it under heavy tree cover, optional over open lawn.

How many downspouts do I need?

Roughly one per 35–40 ft of gutter run, and one at each end of long runs. Undersized downspout counts are why gutters overflow in moderate rain with clean troughs — the water has nowhere to go.

Are gutter guards worth it?

Under trees that drop needles, spinners or heavy leaf litter: yes, quality guards cut cleaning from 2–3 times a year to a rinse. Over open yards with one small tree: a $150 cleaning every year or two beats a $1,000 guard system on pure math.

Seamless or sectional?

Seamless (formed on site from a coil) has joints only at corners — fewer leaks, the professional standard, and the basis of these bands. Sectional vinyl from the home centre is the DIY exception and prices at its band's low end.

🌧️ Where should the downspouts drain TO?

Gutters collect the water; a French drain moves it away from the foundation. The French Drain Estimator sizes gravel, pipe and fabric for the run.

Size the drain

What size gutters do I need?

5-in K-style serves most roofs; 6-in handles steep or large roof planes and long runs, and is the modern default in heavy-rain regions. Oversizing the gutter is cheaper than oversizing the regret.

Can I hang gutters myself?

Sectional vinyl yes; seamless aluminium requires the forming machine, which is why the installed band exists. Fascia condition decides difficulty more than the gutter itself — budget a fascia inspection either way.
