Fence Cost Estimator

Fences are priced by the linear foot, and the material spread is five-to-one. Enter the run and pick the fence: get the installed range with gates and tear-out as separate lines — the two items that ambush every fence budget.

Measure the full run along the property line, corners included. Bands are for the standard 6-ft privacy height (4-ft chain-link and picket run lower). Most suburbs cap backyard fences at 6 ft — check the local rule and any HOA covenant before ordering taller.

More options: gates & tear-out

Tip: dimensions accept tape-measure entries — try 6' 8 5/16" or 6.5.

How it works

Per-foot installed bands at standard height: wood $25–$45, vinyl $40–$65, chain-link $18–$32, aluminium $55–$85, composite $50–$80 per lineal foot. Gates are not fence: budget $250–$450 per standard gate installed (wide drive gates far more). Removing an old fence adds about $8 per lineal foot for tear-out and haul-away. Labour sits near half the total on wood and vinyl; chain-link's cheapness is mostly material.

Estimates only, typical US ranges. Rocky soil, slope, tree roots on the line and permit/HOA processes are the classic quote-growers. Property-line fences demand a survey conversation with the neighbour before a dollar changes hands — the cheapest fence dispute is the one that never starts.

Method & worked example

Method: Total = length × material band (wood $25–45, vinyl $40–65, chain-link $18–32, aluminium $55–85, composite $50–80 per lineal ft) + gates at $250–450 each + optional tear-out at $8 per lineal ft.

Worked example: 100 ft of wood privacy with one gate: $2,500–$4,500 of fence + $250–$450 gate = $2,750–$4,950 installed.

When to use this

  • You are budgeting a yard fence and material choice is still open.
  • A fence quote arrived as one number and you want the anatomy.
  • You are adding gates or an old-fence tear-out to the plan.

How to use it, step by step

  1. Measure the full run along the property line.
  2. Pick the material.
  3. Count the gates honestly — walk gates and any drive gate.
  4. Toggle tear-out if an old fence occupies the line.

Another worked example

150 ft of vinyl privacy with two gates: fence 150 × $40–65 = $6,000–$9,750; gates 2 × $250–450 = $500–$900; total $6,500–$10,650 installed (mid-band rate $52.50/ft). The same run in wood would land near $4,000–$7,650 — the material gap on a long run is a used car.

Frequently asked questions

How much does 100 ft of wood fence cost?

$2,750–$4,950 installed including one gate: $2,500–$4,500 of fence plus $250–$450 for the gate. The same 100 ft in vinyl runs $4,250–$6,950; in chain-link, $2,050–$3,650. Material is destiny in fence pricing.

How much does 150 ft of vinyl fence cost?

With two gates: $6,500–$10,650 installed. Vinyl's mid-band rate is $52.50 per foot against wood's $35 — the gap buys zero staining and wash-clean maintenance for the fence's life.

Wood or vinyl?

Wood: cheaper, repairable board-by-board, needs stain every few years. Vinyl: roughly 50% more up front, washes clean, never stains, but repairs mean whole panels and it can crack on hard impacts in deep cold. The 15-year cost gap is narrower than the quote gap.

Why do gates cost extra?

A gate is a small engineered structure — heavier posts, hardware, a frame that must not sag — hung in a hole in your fence. $250–$450 installed for a walk gate is normal; double drive gates run $800–$2,000+.

Can I save by DIY-ing a fence?

Posts are the job: straight, plumb, at depth, in concrete — rent the auger and it is a very doable two-weekend project, saving most of the labour half. The Concrete Calculator sizes post-hole concrete at about 1–2 bags per post.

How long does a wood fence last?

Pressure-treated pine privacy fencing typically serves 15–20 years with stain maintenance; cedar a few years longer. Posts are the failure point — gravel-set or concrete-set with the crown shedding water is the longevity decision.

Should neighbours split the cost?

Custom varies by state and city (some fence laws presume shared benefit on boundary fences). A written agreement before work beats a customary assumption after the invoice — another reason the itemised total here is useful.
