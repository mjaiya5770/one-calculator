Window Replacement Cost Estimator

Window quotes arrive as one five-figure blur. This estimator prices them the way the industry buys them — per window, by type, with the frame material factored — so a 10-window quote can be checked line by line instead of gasped at.

Prices are per standard-size window, installed (insert replacement into sound frames). Full-frame replacement, structural changes and very large units price above these bands — see the notes.

How it works

Installed per-window bands in vinyl frames: single-hung $450–$750, double-hung $550–$900, sliding $500–$850, casement $650–$1,050, picture $700–$1,200, bay $1,800–$3,200. Fiberglass frames multiply by about 1.2, wood/clad by 1.35 — the premium is material and finish, not installation difficulty. The labour inside each number is roughly a third: that is why one window costs nearly as much per unit as twenty (the truck roll is in every price), and why whole-house quotes per window should sit at or below these bands, never above.

Estimates only, typical US ranges for insert replacements in standard openings. Full-frame work, rotted sills, tempered-glass requirements (near doors and tubs) and historic profiles add real cost. Beware the perpetual "50% off this week only" model — compare per-window totals against this band instead.

Method & worked example

Method: Total = window count × per-window installed band by type (single-hung $450–750, double-hung $550–900, sliding $500–850, casement $650–1,050, picture $700–1,200, bay $1,800–3,200) × frame factor (vinyl 1.0, fiberglass 1.2, wood/clad 1.35).

Worked example: Ten double-hung vinyl windows: 10 × $550–900 = $5,500–$9,000 installed, mid-band $7,250.

When to use this

  • A window company's quote needs a per-window reality check.
  • You are phasing replacements and budgeting this year's rooms.
  • You are comparing window types room by room (casements over the sink, hung elsewhere).

How to use it, step by step

  1. Count the windows in this phase.
  2. Pick the dominant type — re-run per type if the house mixes them.
  3. Apply the frame factor for the material you are quoted.
  4. Compare the per-window line, where padding hides.

Another worked example

Six casement windows in vinyl for a kitchen and bath refresh: 6 × $650–1,050 = $3,900–$6,300, mid-band $5,100. In fiberglass frames the same six would run $4,680–$7,560 — frame choice alone is a four-figure decision on a small project.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost to replace 10 windows?

In double-hung vinyl: $5,500–$9,000 installed, mid-band $7,250 — $550–$900 per window. The same ten in casement run $6,500–$10,500. A quote per window far above the band for standard inserts deserves a line-item explanation.

What do 6 casement windows cost?

$3,900–$6,300 in vinyl. Casements carry more hardware (cranks, hinges, locks) than hung windows, which is most of their premium — and why their seals age better when maintained.

Why is a bay window so much more?

A bay is three windows, a seat, a header structure and often a small roof — $1,800–$3,200 is really a mini construction project with glass in it. Bows (4–5 panels) price higher still.

Vinyl, fiberglass or wood frames?

Vinyl is the value standard and the band basis here. Fiberglass (+20%) handles heat and big sizes better and can be painted. Wood/clad (+35%) is the historic-home and high-end choice. All three insulate similarly at the same glass spec — you are buying looks, longevity and profile, not R-value.

Do new windows pay for themselves in energy?

Rarely on energy alone at these prices — expect single-digit to low-double-digit percent off heating/cooling in a leaky-window house. Comfort, noise, condensation and resale carry the rest of the case. The BTU Estimator shows how much of your load is envelope; windows are one part of it.

🌡️ Chasing the energy bill too?

If drafts sent you window shopping, the BTU Estimator shows the whole envelope story — insulation level included — before you spend five figures on one part of it.

Insert or full-frame replacement?

Inserts (pocket replacements) keep sound frames and trim — faster, cheaper, the basis of these bands. Full-frame is warranted when frames are rotted or the opening itself changes; expect it to price noticeably above the band.

What efficiency rating should I look for?

ENERGY STAR for your climate zone is the sane floor; U-factor at or below 0.30 in heating climates and low SHGC in cooling climates are the spec-sheet numbers that matter more than pane count marketing.
