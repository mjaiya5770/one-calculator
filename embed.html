Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes — free for any website, including commercial sites. There is no paid tier, no watermark and no usage cap.

Do I need to credit One Calculator?

No. Attribution is not required. The widget carries one small “Calculator via One Calculator” line of its own so your readers can find the full library — you do not need to add any link or credit yourself.

Will it slow my site down?

The widget loads on demand inside an iframe and runs entirely in the reader’s browser — no libraries, no trackers, nothing added to your own page weight beyond the iframe itself.

Please use it as-is: do not modify the calculator, restyle it to look like your own product, or remove the small “via” line. You can set the width (100%, 720px or 480px) and adjust the snippet’s height to fit your layout.
